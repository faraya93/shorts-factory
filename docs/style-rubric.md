# Style rubric: does a frame read as the reference pages' look? (owner brief 2026-10-26; derived from docs/reference-style-spec.md)

Scored per hero still and per scene draft gate by READING the rendered frame (and measuring pixels where marked). Each item 1 = pass, 0 = fail.
A frame PASSES at >= 8/10 with no failure in items 1, 2 or 10 (the identity-defining ones).

| # | Criterion | How checked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dark stage: background is the near-black void, no light or pastel stage | measured: border median within dE 6 of #120F10 |
| 2 | PASSIVE objects are grey (no gold on housing, table, idle blade) | read |
| 3 | ONE gold accent, used only on the active quantity (stress, tension, the spring), with a soft bloom | read |
| 4 | Chunky simplified hard-surface model; fine detail only where the fact is (blade section, hook slot, spring) | read |
| 5 | Soft key plus warm rim and a subtle warm halo behind the hero near the top; dark vignette toward the edges | read |
| 6 | Real PBR material response (plastic, rubber, brushed steel), not flat shading, no outlines, no cel bands | read |
| 7 | The camera is moving (orbit, push-in or tracking) and no static hold longer than ~1 s | scene gates only |
| 8 | Top ~25% of the frame left clear for the captions | read |
| 9 | Hero centred in the lower ~70% with large dark negative space | read |
| 10 | Drawability law: hard surfaces only, no people, hands, anatomy, cloth, fluids or animals | read |

Honest limits: I judge stills only (I cannot watch motion), and "reads as the pages' look" is my judgement against the three reference videos' frames, not a measurement.
