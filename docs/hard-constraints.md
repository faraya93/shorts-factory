## 2. The additional finalist: CORAL (best LIGHT direction under the constraints)
Pattern in the owner's reactions: every saturated mid-tone rejected (Esmeralda, Violeta, Cobalto, Fucsia/Frambuesa), every warm light direction liked (Papel, Mandarina, Rosa). So the target was a WARM, LIGHT stage that repairs Papel's measured failures instead of re-arguing them. I searched hue 0-120 degrees x L* 66-94 x chroma 14-60 for warm light stages with dE >= 25 from white, >= 25 from black, ink text >= 7:1 and the fewest failing heroes. Finding: **a cream, butter or sand stage cannot pass C3** (they are near white by definition; Papel's dE 10 is a property of "pale", not a bug). The warm-light region that does pass is coral/salmon and orange-rose; the best compromise on heroes is coral at hue 45, L* 72, chroma 44.

What CORAL does not fix: orange tools, light skin and organ pink (body family) sit within dE 13-16 of it, so body heroes should stand in a cream cutaway plate; the teal accent is dark, so the "glow" reads by hue not by brightness; it is close in spirit to MANDARINA (which fails C1/C2) but lighter and pinker. A "Rosa+" (Rosa deepened by about 2 L*) would also pass C3; I did not build it because the brief asked for one additional finalist.
