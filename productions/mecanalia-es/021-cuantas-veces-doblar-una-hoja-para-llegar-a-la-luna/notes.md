## Script (8 scenes, family numbers, variant scale, 154 words, ~53.1 s at 2.9 w/s; band 50-60 s)
Hook: ¿Cuántas veces tendrías que doblar una hoja de papel para llegar a la Luna? | false belief: Harían falta miles o millones de dobleces. | turn: Scene 7: on paper it takes 42 folds, but in real life nobody has passed 12 (Britney Gallivan, 2002, with a strip more than a kilometre long). | reframe: No hacen falta millones de dobleces: cada uno duplica todo lo anterior. | share archetype: ese amigo que, cuando le cuentas esto, saca una hoja para intentarlo
Theme / main tension (TOPIC BALANCE): paper folding / enormous.

1. **hook** (~7.2 s): ¿Cuántas veces tendrías que doblar una hoja de papel para llegar a la Luna? Seguro piensas que miles. Son muchísimas menos.
   - What changes in the world: A single white sheet lies on flat ground at dusk with the Moon low on the horizon; tag ¿MILES? appears; the sheet folds in half once.
   - Transformation: hoja extendida en el suelo → se dobla por primera vez; camera: empuje bajo desde la Luna en el horizonte hasta la hoja
   - Overlay: tag ¿MILES? at 'miles.'
   - Element: sheet of paper: route build, geometric (thin white slab with a soft fold); risk low; aliases (fixed now, before any reading): paper, sheet of paper, paper sheet, hoja, page
2. **duplica** (~6.9 s): Una hoja mide una décima de milímetro. Cada vez que la doblas, su grosor se duplica: dos hojas, cuatro, ocho.
   - What changes in the world: Macro on the edge of the stack: one layer becomes two, four, eight as each fold lands; a counter doubles in step.
   - Transformation: una capa → dos → cuatro → ocho capas; camera: primer plano lateral del canto que sube con cada doblez
   - Overlay: counter 0,1 mm (mono) at 'décima'
   - Overlay: counter ×2 at 'duplica:'
   - Element: stack of paper: route build, geometric (layered slabs); risk low; aliases (fixed now, before any reading): paper, stack of paper, paper stack, ream, papers
3. **persona** (~4.1 s): Al doblez catorce, la pila ya es más alta que una persona.
   - What changes in the world: Pull-back: the stack shoots up to 1.6 m and stands beside a person, who looks up at it.
   - Transformation: pila pequeña → pila más alta que una persona; camera: retroceso a plano medio junto a la persona
   - Overlay: counter 14 DOBLECES at 'catorce,'
   - Overlay: counter 1,6 m at 'persona.'
   - Element: person standing beside a stack of paper: route library human-figure, sealed pose standing (lone figure: default outfit); risk low (sealed standing 85/85/60); aliases (fixed now, before any reading): person, human figure, figure, mannequin, man, woman
4. **montana** (~4.8 s): Al doblez veintisiete, pasa la cima del Everest, la montaña más alta del mundo.
   - What changes in the world: The stack keeps growing; the camera climbs alongside it past a snow-capped mountain until the column rises above the summit and the clouds.
   - Transformation: pila a ras de suelo → la pila supera la cima nevada; camera: subida vertical pegada a la pila, la montaña pasa de lado
   - Overlay: counter 27 at 'veintisiete,'
   - Overlay: counter 13 km at 'cima'
   - Element: snow-capped mountain: route library terrain (BlenderKit RF mountain terrains, 400-700 MB; judged heavy) or a stylised build: rock peak with a snow cap material; risk medium: a stylised peak is iconic but organic; context = clouds and the stack beside it; aliases (fixed now, before any reading): mountain, mountain peak, Everest, peak, montaña
5. **espacio** (~5.9 s): Al doblez treinta y dos, ya está en el espacio, más arriba de donde orbitan los astronautas.
   - What changes in the world: The sky darkens from blue to black and stars appear while the column keeps climbing; the curve of the horizon falls away below.
   - Transformation: cielo azul → cielo negro con estrellas mientras la pila sigue subiendo; camera: retroceso lateral amplio que muestra la columna entera atravesando el cielo hasta el negro
   - Overlay: counter 32 at 'treinta'
   - Overlay: counter 430 km at 'espacio,'
   - Element: paper column rising into space: route stack build + world sky gradient and star field (shader); risk medium: the column must still read as paper; its layered edge stays in frame; aliases (fixed now, before any reading): paper, stack of paper, paper stack, column of paper
6. **luna** (~5.5 s): Y al doblez cuarenta y dos, supera los trescientos ochenta mil kilómetros: llega a la Luna.
   - What changes in the world: The Moon grows in frame as the column's tip reaches it and passes its distance; the camera swings around the Moon to look back down the thin column.
   - Transformation: Luna lejana → la punta de la pila llega a la Luna; camera: órbita alrededor de la Luna mirando hacia la pila
   - Overlay: counter 42 at 'cuarenta'
   - Overlay: counter 384 400 km at 'kilómetros:'
   - Element: the Moon: route library BlenderKit 'Moon' by Cyberking 42 (4611d12e, CC0, 50.7 MB; thumbnail not viewable from search, judge at the asset step) or a build: grey sphere with a crater material; risk low-medium: a grey cratered sphere in black space is iconic; aliases (fixed now, before any reading): moon, the moon, luna, lunar
7. **giro** (~9.0 s): Pero en la vida real, nadie ha pasado de doce. En 2002, una estudiante lo logró con una tira de papel de más de un kilómetro.
   - What changes in the world: The turn: the column collapses back to the ground; a very long paper strip runs to the horizon and a student, at its end, finishes the twelfth fold of a small thick wad.
   - Transformation: columna hasta la Luna → se desploma y queda una tira larguísima doblada doce veces; camera: travelling largo a lo largo de la tira hasta la estudiante
   - Overlay: counter 12 at 'doce.'
   - Overlay: chip 2002 at '2002,'
   - Overlay: chip 1,2 km at 'kilómetro.'
   - Element: person beside a long strip of folded paper: route library human-figure, sealed pose standing (a different person: distinct wardrobe); strip geometric; risk low-medium: the strip must read as paper; the wad is the cue; aliases (fixed now, before any reading): person, human figure, figure, mannequin, woman, man
8. **cierre** (~9.7 s): Así que no hacen falta millones de dobleces: cada uno duplica todo lo anterior. Envíaselo a ese amigo que, cuando le cuentas esto, saca una hoja para intentarlo.
   - What changes in the world: Single reframe image: one sheet folds once more and the doubling replays as a quick climb from the ground to the Moon in one continuous move.
   - Transformation: una hoja → un doblez más y la pila vuelve a subir hasta la Luna; camera: subida continua de la hoja a la Luna
   - Overlay: ENVÍASELO tag at_word 'envíaselo'

Staging mode: world (proposal for production; the design lens confirms it at the beat sheet). the payoff is height: the stack climbs out of a landscape, past a mountain, through the sky into black space to the Moon; only a modelled world gives the camera something to climb past (reference objects, format policy 'Dynamism').

## Scientific simplifications (stated plainly)
- Folding is idealised: real paper cannot be folded past about a dozen times, which is the turn.
- The column is drawn as a thin straight tower; it would really be a wad as wide as the last fold allows.
